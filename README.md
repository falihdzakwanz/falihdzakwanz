I'm a **Fullstack Developer** who believes great software comes out of boring problems: processes still done on paper, data scattered across spreadsheets, and reports people wait days for.

- 🏗️ **I ship real systems, not exercises.** Led a team of 3 engineers to replace a paper-based campus document workflow with a single platform — processing time dropped from **7 days to 3 days**, with a **91.3% UAT score from 111 respondents**.
- 🗺️ **Location data belongs on a map.** I build GIS-backed web systems with **PostGIS**, so national assets live on a map instead of in a spreadsheet.
- 🤖 **AI with receipts.** My approach to chatbots is **RAG** — answers pulled from documents, not guesswork.
- 🎓 **Teaching sharpens the craft.** 10 months as a teaching assistant for Algorithms & Data Structures, mentoring **100+ students**.
- 🤝 Open to roles in **public sector, education, and AI** — from database design all the way to production.
